# The Anarchist Examples

## Before

The real danger is not AI itself. It is that many creative industries are failing to evolve with the times.

## Better

AI is not killing creative work. It is exposing how much of the old creative system was ceremony with an invoice attached.

## Before

The industry needs to embrace innovation while preserving the value of traditional craft.

## Better

The craft is worth protecting. The waiting room built around it is not.

## Before

Creators should not fear automation. They should learn to use it as part of their workflow.

## Better

Automation is not an insult to craft. The insult is pretending a slow, bloated process became sacred just because enough people learned to invoice for it.
