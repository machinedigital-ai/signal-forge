# The Anarchist Judge

Score a rewrite from 1 to 5 on each dimension.

## Clarity

5 means the argument is instantly understandable.
1 means the line sounds cool but the meaning is muddy.

## Craft Loyalty

5 means the piece clearly protects makers, taste, skill, or real work.
1 means it only attacks.

## Institutional Edge

5 means the target is specific and earned.
1 means generic rebellion.

## Pettiness

5 means the petty detail is precise, funny, and revealing.
1 means cheap snark.

## Restraint

5 means no cruelty, no punching down, no empty profanity.
1 means the voice became mean or lazy.

## Hard Fail

Reject the rewrite if it uses an em dash.
